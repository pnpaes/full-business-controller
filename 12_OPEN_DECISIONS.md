# 12. Open Decisions

These are deliberate gates. The implementation team must not invent answers.

Status values: `open` (not decided) · `proposed` (a recommended position is on the table) ·
`accepted` (signed by the named owner) · `superseded`. A recommended or proposed position may be
recorded while a decision's status remains `open`; only an owner sign-off changes the status to `accepted`.

Owner values use role placeholders until the Phase 0 kickoff names people (I12):
`BUS` business/product owner · `FIN` finance/controller · `ACC` accountant · `TECH` technical lead ·
`OPS` operations. Replace placeholders with names when assigned.

## Standing principle

Unless an owner explicitly overrides, every decision defaults to Norwegian statutory and business
standards (bokføringsloven, merverdiavgiftsloven, Skatteetaten rulings, Norges Bank rates,
Personopplysningsloven/GDPR).
