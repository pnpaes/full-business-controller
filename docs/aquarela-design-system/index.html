05 / States & behavior

Design the moments between.

Empty, loading, error, and confirmation states belong in the system from day one.

Empty

No deliveries yet

New deliveries will appear here once recorded.

Loading specimen

Fetching inventory

Keep the layout stable. Announce loading once.

Error

Inventory unavailable

We couldn’t load stock levels. Your changes are still saved.

Keep consequential actions deliberate.
Refunds, stock losses, and day closing need a clear summary and explicit confirmation. Never make a toast the only record of a failed save.
06 / Apply the system

Keep your app. Change its language.

Designed as a theme and recipes for your existing shadcn/ui implementation.

1
Apply semantic colors

Merge shadcn-theme.css after existing theme definitions. Keep your imports, routing, and component behavior.

2
Connect Tailwind tokens

Use the v4 mapping or the v3 extension supplied. Choose one according to the app’s installed version.

3
Standardize component recipes

Use 40 px controls, 16 px card corners, fine borders, and dark primary actions. Extend shared components once.

4
Verify real coffee-shop workflows

Check stock edits, long product names, currency, keyboard navigation, validation, and narrow screens.

Semantic, not screen-specific

<Button>Save product</Button>

<Badge variant="warning">

<Card>

The theme handles color. Shared recipes handle proportion. Existing primitives retain interaction and accessibility.
